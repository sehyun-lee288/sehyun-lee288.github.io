Sehyun Lee

Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence · KAIST

I am a Ph.D. student at the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Jaesik Choi in the Statistical Artificial Intelligence Lab.

I study how neural networks represent concepts and use multimodal information. My research focuses on interpretability, including concept circuits and polysemantic features, and multimodal language models.

I have worked on multimodal model evaluation at NAVER Cloud and industrial time-series modeling at Gauss Labs and INEEJI. I am interested in postdoctoral and research opportunities.
